How to start & coach’s guide

Start at level 2. Choose a piece from the tray. The gold dot marks one filled square on that piece. Tap the board square where that dot should land. If any part overlaps or sticks outside the frame, it stays in the tray.

Levels 1–3 use rotations; levels 4–6 add flips. The frame and number of pieces grow. Pieces can fit locally but block later pieces: pick one up and try another arrangement. Every new puzzle has at least one solution; other complete arrangements are accepted.

Use Show a guide to see a placement for the selected piece. If another piece blocks it, pick that piece up first. Try 15–20 minutes, then let Zach explain a strategy or coach you through a harder puzzle.

This is a spatial puzzle with fossil-themed tiles, not a reconstruction of dinosaur anatomy. Completed levels save on this browser; refreshing starts a new puzzle.
