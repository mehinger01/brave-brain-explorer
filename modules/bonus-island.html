Coach’s field guide

Start at any level. Ask “What do you notice?” and “What will you try?” before helping. All six levels are available immediately. Repeat a challenge with New Puzzle; its difficulty stays the same.

For 45–60 minutes: briefing (5), Rescue Route (15), Track Detective (10), Signal Repair (10), then a harder replay and case wrap-up (5–15). A hint or reset never removes an earned clue.

Extend it: ask Zach to explain which evidence rules out each turtle, plan the whole route before moving, or sketch a signal for you to remember. Progress saves on this browser when storage is available.

🐢 Pause and reset

Feet on the floor. Drop your shoulders. Take three slow breaths. Say: “I can change my plan.” Then choose one next step.

Hard means new. A clue is earned by finishing, even with help.
