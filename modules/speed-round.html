Coach’s quick guide

Level 3 is the starting point for a bright second grader. Move up if he can explain his strategy comfortably. Levels 5–6 are stretch challenges; let him choose a lower level whenever needed.

Read rules aloud together. Ask “Which clue matters?” or “Can you rule anything out?” Untimed play builds the strategy first. The optional clock counts thinking time only: it pauses after a correct answer, when you pause, and when the browser tab is hidden.

Try 8–12 minutes per game, then let him choose a harder replay or coach you through a round. Mistakes give feedback and another attempt. Hints are welcome; no points are taken away.
