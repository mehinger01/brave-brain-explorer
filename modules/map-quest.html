🗺️ Map Quest

Scout the route. Find the keys. Bring the forest friends home.

Choose your expedition

Ten levels, from a short trail to a sprawling labyrinth. Every level is open. Start at level 3 for keys and gates, or level 1 to learn the controls.

How to play & coach’s guide

Use arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen arrows, or tap a neighboring square. Walls block movement. Collect a key to unlock its matching gate automatically. Visit every animal friend, then reach 🏁 camp.

There is no timer. Ask “Where should we go first?” and “Which dead end tells us something?” Move up when the route feels comfortable. Levels 5–10 require planning around multiple gates and rescue stops.

Harder mazes extend beyond the viewing window. Zoom out to scout; zoom in to navigate. Keep the trail visible to track explored paths, or hide it for a memory challenge. A hint marks just one next step toward a reachable objective.
