How to start & coach’s guide

Start with the pipe touching 💧. Its opening must meet the next pipe’s opening. Straight pipes have two useful positions; elbows have four. A test stops at the first broken connection and outlines the pipe to work on.

Try level 2 first. Levels grow from 3×3 to 8×8, with longer routes and more checkpoints. Ask “Where will the water stop?” before testing. Spend 10–15 minutes here, then replay a harder puzzle if he wants.

This is a connection puzzle, with a simplified model of water flow. No time limit; hints and retries are welcome. Refreshing makes a new puzzle; completed levels save on this browser.

Good engineers test an idea, notice what happens, and adjust.
