🐢 Turtle Reset Steps
  1. Notice the feeling — are you getting frustrated?
  2. Say "I need a turtle moment."
  3. Take a slow, deep breath in... and out.
  4. Count to 5 in your head.
  5. Ask yourself: "What can I try next?"
